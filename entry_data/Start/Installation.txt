<para><bold>Non Mac-OS Users</bold>

<para>    <bold>Dependencies</bold>
        <bold>-</bold> LLVM (19.1.7_1+)
</para>

You will be required to compile from source with the following commands:
<code>git clone https://github.com/Tundra-t4/Tundra/
cd Tundra
bash assemble.sh <your operating system>
</code>
note: assemble.sh may require sudo depending on your operating system

<bold>Mac-OS Users</bold>
You may download the corresponding tar.gz from <url>https://github.com/Tundra-t4/Tundra/releases<urltext>here</url>

<bold>Installation</bold>
Simply with the .tar.gz run:
<code>mkdir /usr/local/bin/Tundra
tar -xvzf <path to Tundra-2025b-<your operating system>.tar.gz> -C /usr/local/bin/Tundra/
</code>
lastly, you will need to add the folder to PATH for macos users this is navigating to <bold>~/.zshrc</bold> (or your preferred shell) and adding the line:
<code>export PATH="/usr/local/bin/Tundra:$PATH"
</code>
Verify the installation with:
<code>Tundrac version: 
Tundra c(ompiler): Anthology no. 2025b (Arcadia)
</code>

</para>
